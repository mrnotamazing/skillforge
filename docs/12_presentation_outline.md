## Slides to cut if time is short
17 (fold into 18) and 21's scaling detail. **Never cut 3, 6, 13, 19 or 20** — those four are what separate this from an HR-tech pitch.

## Delivery notes
- **Slide 3 is a credibility investment.** Declaring what you won't claim, before you claim anything, buys you the room.
- **Slide 6 must land in silence.** Say the line, then stop for two full seconds.
- **Make the mistake in the demo.** A demo where you perform perfectly proves nothing. Getting caught by your own system proves the system works.
- **Slide 20 is for the professor.** Ending on falsifiability, not on a hockey-stick, is the whole posture of the project.
